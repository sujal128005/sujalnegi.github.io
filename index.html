Limen

Agentic procurement marketplace for industrial tenders

LiveAug 2026 to presentWinner, IBM SkillsBuild South Region Finale
Open live app Read the code
  • Built a multi-agent tender marketplace to compress a 2 to 3 month manual procurement cycle into days.
  • Evaluates suppliers on cost, specs and availability, with sealed bidding, escrow settlement and reputation scoring.
  • Enforces buyer ceilings and seller floors in a Solidity contract, so no agent can lift its own spending limit.
  • Splits raise, approve and pay across three role-gated desks, so no one account can both create and settle a request.
  • The LLM handles text only. Every figure comes from a deterministic engine, verified by 539 automated checks.

Solidity, Node.js, React, LLM integration

How I operate.

Four habits I keep coming back to, whether the thing is a drone, a marketplace or a student club.

Spend only after the proof

On Navyam, hardware spend waited until the drone hit a 0.5 m docking milestone in simulation. Cheap proof first, expensive parts second.

Separate who asks from who pays

Limen splits raise, approve and pay across three role-gated desks, the same control a good purchasing team uses, built into software.

Let the model talk, let the rules decide

In Limen and Khata the AI writes the words and a deterministic engine owns the facts, backed by 863 automated checks across both.

Build toward a customer

Navya has a risk register and a go-to-market plan, not just a demo. Limen was pitched to IBM judges and placed in the top 4 of 25 finalists.
